Fill in every field of the framing. A field that does not apply is an empty string, 0, or "none" (offset: -1).

- subject: what the claim is about, in a few words ("vehicle production", "robotaxi cities", "data-center revenue", "Blackwell shipments", "stock split").
- metric: only when the target is about the whole company's reported revenue, gross or operating margin, operating income or expenses, EPS or capital expenditures. Segment, product, unit, user and operating figures have metric null.
- target_kind:
  - multiple for "double", "triple", "10x";
  - absolute for a level ("$10 billion", "10 cities", "1 million vehicles a year");
  - change for a percentage change ("grow 20%"; a cut is negative: -15);
  - threshold for crossing a line ("profitable", "cash-flow positive", "break even": target_value 0);
  - event for a concrete, observable event with a named product, plant, deal or date: "Blackwell will ship in the fourth quarter", "the stock split takes effect June 10", "production of the Cybercab starts in 2026";
  - none when nothing checkable is promised: aspirations, opinions, strategy, "demand will stay strong", "poised for growth", "AI will transform industries", "we are focused on".
- target_text and target_value: the target exactly as the quote writes it, and as a number (2 for "double", 10 for "10 cities", 2000000000 for "$2 billion", 20 for "20%", 0 for "profitable"). Use only numbers written in the quote or paragraph.
- unit: usd, pct, x (multiples), or the counted thing in one plural word ("cities", "vehicles"). measure_period: quarter or year when the target is a quarterly or annual figure. direction: at_most for a ceiling ("keep losses under $1 billion").
- event: for an event, one short sentence with the company and the exact thing the quote names: the product and model, the partner, the place ("Acme and Globex ship laptops with the Zeta X200 chip", "Initech offers Zeta X200 instances in its Cloudline service", "Initech opens its Ohio plant"). Never a broader category ("new GPUs", "new products"), and nothing the quote does not say: no purposes or comparisons ("as an alternative to ...").
- related_companies: the other companies the claim names as partners, customers, suppliers or makers of the product ("Acme", "Globex"), as the quote names them; empty when none.
- comparison_basis, for a change or a target measured against an earlier figure: yoy when it is against the same period a year earlier ("year over year", "from a year ago"), sequential when against the period before ("from last quarter", "sequentially"), absolute when the target is a level, not a change; none otherwise.
- baseline_text and baseline_value: only when the quote or paragraph states what the target is measured from. Never from memory; otherwise null.
- deadline: the phrase as written and its structure: type; amount and unit for a duration; year when a year is named (four digits; a fiscal year as the company names it); offset when said relative to the statement (0 this, 1 next); quarter, half, month, day or season when named. Do not compute dates:
  - "within a year", "in 18 months": duration with amount and unit;
  - "by the end of next year", "next year", "in 2026", "later this year": calendar_year with year, or offset (0 this year, 1 next);
  - "next fiscal year", "fiscal 2026", "this fiscal year": fiscal_year with year (as the company names it) or offset;
  - "next quarter", "this quarter", "the fiscal fourth quarter", "Q2 fiscal 2025": fiscal_quarter with quarter and year or offset; a calendar quarter ("Q2 calendar 2024", "by Q3 2026" of a calendar year) is calendar_quarter;
  - "by June", "in March 2026": month; "June 10": date; "next summer": season with offset 1;
  - relative deadlines: "next month", "effective next month": month with offset 1 and month 0; "this month", "later this month": month with offset 0; "this quarter", "the current quarter": fiscal_quarter with offset 0; "next quarter": fiscal_quarter with offset 1; "within weeks", "in the coming weeks", "in a few weeks": duration of 8 weeks; "in the coming months", "within months": duration of 6 months; "in the coming days": duration of 2 weeks;
  - a launch, availability, payment or effective date the quote or paragraph states ("goes on sale January 17", "available next week", "effective June 7"): date, month or duration as written;
  - "soon", "shortly", "in the near future": duration of 6 months; "in the near term", "within a year or so": duration of 12 months; "in the coming years", "within the next few years": duration of 3 years;
  - none only when the claim has no time reference at all, or only "over time", "eventually", "in the long run", "someday". Every implied deadline gets a date: "later this year", a stated launch date, "in the coming weeks", "next month", "this quarter".
- new_market_or_product: true when the claim is about entering a new market or selling a product the company does not sell yet.

A claim restating results already reported, or describing the present, has target_kind none and deadline none.
