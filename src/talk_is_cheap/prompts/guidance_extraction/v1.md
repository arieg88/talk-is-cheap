## What counts as guidance

A statement of what the company expects a financial measure of its own to be in a future period: revenue, margins, expenses, operating income, earnings per share, capital expenditures, tax rate, and other measures (segment revenue, growth of a revenue stream, cash flow, share count, deliveries, deployments).

Include:
- Ranges, single figures, plus-or-minus figures and bounds ("at least $90 billion").
- Growth guidance: year-over-year growth is measure `yoy_growth`, change from the previous quarter `sequential_growth`. A growth rate stated alongside a dollar range ("between $197.0 billion and $202.0 billion, or to grow between 9% and 12%") is a separate item. So is the growth implied at the midpoint when the release states it ("the mid-point of the revenue range represents year-over-year growth of approximately 41%").
- Figures given as a share of revenue ("capital expenditures approximately 1.5% of revenue"): measure `share_of_revenue`.
- Targets the guidance assumes for the company's own measures in the target period ("Targets assume non-GAAP operating margin of ~44.0%, GAAP tax rate of ~22.0% ... and diluted share count of ~389 million"): one item each.
- Qualitative guidance about a measure in a period, without a figure ("we expect to deliver operating income this year that is above 2025 operating income", "Reality Labs operating losses remaining similar to 2025 levels"): `value_text` null, the expectation in `qualitative`.
- GAAP and non-GAAP figures from reconciliation tables of guidance when the table gives a figure not stated elsewhere, and repeats of the same figure in another section (each repeat is its own item; duplicates are merged later).

Do not include:
- Results already reported, comparisons with past results ("compared with $17.4 billion in third quarter 2025"), or how results compared with earlier guidance.
- Earlier guidance mentioned for comparison ("narrowed from our prior outlook of $125-145 billion"); only the current figure is an item.
- Reconciling adjustments between GAAP and non-GAAP figures (stock-based compensation per share, amortization of intangibles, income tax effects).
- Assumptions about outside factors (exchange rates, tax law, the economy, acquisitions closing): put them in `conditions` of the items they apply to. This includes the exchange rates a figure is based on ("an operating margin of 31.5% for 2026 based on F/X rates as of January 1, 2026" is a margin item with that condition). `constant_currency` is only for figures stated in constant currency or F/X neutral.
- A rate used over the long term to compute other guidance ("our non-GAAP tax provision uses a long-term projected tax rate of 22.0%"): a condition of the items it is used for, not an item.
- Product plans and timelines, strategy, and statements without a measure and a period ("we will maintain a strong balance sheet", "Cybercab is on schedule for volume production starting in 2026").

## How to fill each item

- One item per metric, basis, segment, measure, currency basis and period. "GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 74.9% and 75.0%, respectively, plus or minus 50 basis points" is two items, with `value_text` "74.9%, plus or minus 50 basis points" and "75.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points". "GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates are expected to be 17.0%, plus or minus 1%" is two items with the same figure. Growth "in constant currency and USD" is two items, one with `constant_currency` true.
- `metric`: `revenue` for total revenue or net sales (segment revenue too, with `segment`); `gross_margin`, `operating_margin` and `tax_rate` for those rates; `operating_expenses` for operating expenses or total expenses; `operating_income`, `eps` (diluted earnings per share) and `capex` (capital expenditures); `other` for anything else (other income and expense, ARR, remaining performance obligation, cash flow, share count, deliveries).
- `basis`: as labelled in the text. A measure without a GAAP or non-GAAP label is `unspecified`.
- A statement about several quarters ("in Q3 and Q4", "in the second half of the year", "for the remaining quarters of 2026") is one item per quarter, with `period_text` naming that quarter ("third quarter of 2026").
- `period_text`: the period as the text names it, taken from the heading or lead-in sentence when the item itself does not name it ("Outlook for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 is as follows:" gives "third quarter of fiscal 2027"). Use the release's words; do not convert them to dates or labels.
- `value_text`: only this item's figure, with its own range or plus-or-minus words and units, as written. Keep the unit words ("billion", "%", "$"). When one phrase gives figures for several items, write the part that belongs to this item with the words it shares. Keep descriptive figures as written ("in the mid-70% range", "slightly above 12%").
- `conditions`: every assumption, inclusion or exclusion stated for the item, and those stated for all of the guidance in these sections ("not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China", "excluding any discrete items", "absent any changes to our tax landscape", "assumes no additional business acquisitions, restructurings, or legal settlements"). Null only when none is stated.
- `quote`: the exact text of the sentence, bullet or table row the item comes from, copied character for character, at most a few hundred characters. For a table row, copy the cells of that row as they appear, separated by ` | `.
