For every evidence item, one finding in `assessments`:
- satisfies: the item itself shows the rule was met: the event happened, or the figure reached the target. A plan, schedule, forecast or expectation does not satisfy the rule; only what happened does.
- partly: the item shows the rule partly met: a figure moved toward the target without reaching it, or part of an event happened (say which part in partial_reason).
- contradicts: the item shows the rule was not met in the period it is about (the period was reported and the figure or event fell short).
- walks_back: a later statement by the company withdraws, delays or lowers the claim.
- irrelevant: anything else, including items about other products, periods or companies.

The item must be about the claim as it was made: the same product, model, partner, deal, place or measure the rule names.
