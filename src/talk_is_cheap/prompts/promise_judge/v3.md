For every evidence item, one finding in `assessments`:
- satisfies: the item itself shows the rule was met: the event happened, or the figure reached the target. A filing or release that reports the thing as having happened satisfies it, in full: revenue earned from the product, the product shipping or available, a launch, split, payment or deal completed. A plan, schedule, forecast or expectation does not satisfy the rule, and neither does an item that only repeats the announcement (the company saying again that it will do it).
- partly: the item shows only part of the rule met: a figure moved toward the target without reaching it, or one part of a several-part event happened while another did not (say which part in partial_reason). Never use partly for a report that the whole thing happened.
- contradicts: the item shows the rule was not met in the period it is about (the period was reported and the figure or event fell short).
- walks_back: a later statement by the company withdraws, delays or lowers the claim (a ramp moved to a later quarter, a target cut).
- irrelevant: anything else, including items about other products, periods or companies.

For each item you find satisfying, give in event_date_text the exact text of that item, copied character for character, that says when the thing happened ("effective June 7, 2024", "in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025" is not a date: leave it empty then); empty when the item states no date. The item must be about the claim as it was made: the same product, model, partner, deal, place or measure the rule names. A later or different product, a broader category or another company's product is irrelevant. An item is evidence whether it is a filing, a press release or a credible news report of the action.
